The heavy blast doors at the base of the Vance Spire didn't open. They melted.
A wave of grey, ash-like energy dissolved the four-foot-thick durasteel as if it were sugar in
water.

Through

the

shimmering

heat

walked

a

man

who

looked

more

like

a

corpse

than

a

hunter.

Inquisitor

Voren

was

tall

and

skeletal,

wearing

the

long,

high-collared

black

duster

of

the

Council's

Internal

Affairs.

He didn't carry a weapon. He didn't need one. As he stepped onto the training floor, the violet
runes

in

the

alloy

floor

dimmed

and

died.

His

very

presence

was

an

affront

to

the

ambient

mana

of

the

world.

"Elara Vance," Voren’s voice was a dry, papery whisper that somehow reached the observation
deck.

"Your

family

has

always

been

fond

of

forbidden

toys.

But

harboring

an

Unregistered

Anomaly?

That

is

a

sin

even

your

name

cannot

cover."

Elara stood at the railing, her face as white as her combat suit. "Inquisitor Voren. You have no
warrant

for

this

facility."

"I am the warrant," Voren said. He stopped in the center of the floor, his eyes—hollow pits of
grey—turning

toward

Kaelen.

He

tilted

his

head

like

a

curious

bird.

"You.

The

Null

who

isn't

a

Null.

I

touched

the

iron

bars

of

the

Pit,

boy.

I

saw

the

memory

of

your

'Blue

Fire.'

It

felt...

hungry.

Like

a

void.

Tell

me,

where

did

you

find

the

System?"

Kaelen’s heart skipped a beat. The Inquisitor had said the word. System.
"You know about it," Kaelen said, stepping forward. He felt the Void Vanguard energy rising in
his

chest,

an

icy

pressure

that

wanted

to

be

let

out.

"The Council has spent twenty years hunting the remnants of the 2006 Anomaly," Voren said, a
thin,

cruel

smile

touching

his

lips.

"We

know

of

the

'Glitches'

that

occasionally

appear.

You

are

the

most

advanced

one

I’ve

seen.

I’m

going

to

enjoy

pulling

the

code

out

of

your

marrow."
