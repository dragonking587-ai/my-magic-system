Sleep did not come easily in the silent heights of the Vance Estate. For Kaelen, the silence was
louder

than

the

machinery

of

the

mines.

Every

time

he

closed

his

eyes,

he

saw

the

blue

light

of

the

System

burning

behind

his

eyelids,

and

every

time

he

drifted

off,

he

felt

the

spectral

weight

of

the

Spectral

Blade

in

his

palm.

At 4:00 AM, the lights in his suite hummed to life, shifting from a soft amber to a clinical, piercing
white.

"Rise and shine, cousin," Elara’s voice crackled over the room’s intercom.
Kaelen was already on his feet, his hand instinctively reaching for a weapon that wasn't there.
He

stood

in

the

center

of

the

room,

shirtless,

his

lean

muscles

mapping

the

story

of

a

decade

of

labor.

"I’m awake," he said.
"Dress in the gear provided in the vanity," Elara commanded. "We leave in ten minutes. Today,
the

world

officially

forgets

that

Kaelen

the

Null

ever

existed."

In the vanity, he found a suit of charcoal-grey combat fatigues. The fabric was thin but felt as
strong

as

Kevlar,

woven

with

mana-conductive

threads

that

shimmered

when

he

moved.

As

he

pulled

the

boots

on,

he

checked

the

Ring

of

the

Silent

Pulse.

It

sat

heavy

on

his

finger,

its

black

surface

absorbing

the

room's

light.

He caught his reflection in the full-length mirror. He didn't recognize the man staring back. The
sunken

cheeks

were

filling

out,

and

the

"miner's

slouch"—that

permanent

curve

of

the

spine

from

working

in

low

tunnels—was

gone.

He

looked

like

a

soldier.

He

looked

like

an

Awakened.

He stepped into the hallway, where Elara was waiting. She looked tired, the dark circles under
her

eyes

betraying

a

night

spent

scrubbing

his

existence

from

the

global

databases.

"From this moment on," she said, her voice low and sharp, "you are Kaelen Vance. You were
raised

in

the

Southern

Badlands,

tutored

in

private

due

to

a

fragile

mana-constitution.

Your

'Awakening'

happened

three

days

ago

during

a

sparring

accident.

If

anyone

asks

about

the

mines,

you

tell

them

you’ve

never

seen

a

crystal

that

wasn't

set

in

a

ring.

Understood?"

"I can play the part," Kaelen said. "But the people I worked with... Silas... they know who I am."
"Silas and the others have been 'relocated' to a safer sector with a lifetime pension," Elara said,
her

silver

eyes

flashing.

"I

don't

leave

loose

ends,

Kaelen.

I

can't

afford

to."
