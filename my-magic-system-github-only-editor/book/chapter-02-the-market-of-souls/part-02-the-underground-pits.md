Kaelen emerged from the alley and headed toward the industrial district. He needed credits, and
he

needed

them

fast.

The

50

credits

from

his

breakout

wouldn't

even

buy

him

a

decent

meal

in

the

Grey

Zone,

let

alone

the

gear

he

needed

to

survive

the

inevitable

hunt.

Following the advice of an old miner named Silas, he found his way to a crumbling textile
warehouse.

The

windows

were

boarded

up

with

mana-suppressant

lead,

and

two

massive

men

with

"Earth

Aspect"

skin

stood

by

the

entrance.

"Entry is five credits, Null," one of them grunted, his eyes glowing a dull, stony brown.
Kaelen handed over the chips he had scavenged from Barrett's locker earlier that day. The
guard

scanned

him

with

a

handheld

device.

It

beeped

green.

"Flatline," the guard chuckled to his partner. "Another Null looking to get his head kicked in for a
paycheck.

Go

on

in,

kid.

Try

not

to

die

on

the

floor;

it’s

a

pain

to

mop

up."

Kaelen ignored the jibe and stepped inside.
The interior of the warehouse had been hollowed out to create a primitive coliseum. In the
center

was

a

cage

made

of

reinforced

iron

bars,

and

surrounding

it

were

hundreds

of

people—mostly

Nulls

and

low-tier

Awakened—screaming,

betting,

and

drinking.

The

air

was

thick

with

the

smell

of

sweat,

cheap

booze,

and

the

metallic

tang

of

blood.

This was the Underground Pit, the dark mirror of the global Apex Games. Here, there were no
referees

and

no

mercy.

You

fought

until

your

opponent

stopped

moving,

and

you

got

paid

in

proportion

to

the

"Tier

Gap"

of

the

victory.

Kaelen walked toward the registration desk. A woman with silver-gray hair sat there, her eyes
sharp

and

cold.

This

was

Elara

Vance.

At

the

time,

Kaelen

didn't

know

her

name;

he

only

knew

she

looked

out

of

place.

Her

clothes

were

too

clean,

her

posture

too

regal

for

a

blood-pit.

"Name?" she asked, not looking up from her data-slate.
"Kaelen."
"Aspect?"
"None," Kaelen replied.
She paused, her stylus hovering over the screen. She finally looked up, her silver eyes scanning
him

with

a

professional

intensity.

"A

Null?

We’ve

already

had

three

deaths

tonight,

Kaelen.

The

crowd

doesn't

want

to

see

a

slaughter;

they

want

to

see

a

fight.

If

you

go

in

there,

the

Pit

Master

isn't

responsible

for

what's

left

of

you."

"I know the risks," Kaelen said.
Elara stared at him for a long beat. She saw something in his eyes—not the desperation she
usually

saw

in

Nulls,

but

a

terrifying,

calm

certainty.

"Fine," she sighed, typing into the slate. "You're in the next bracket. You're fighting The Butcher.
If

you

survive

three

minutes,

you

get

100

credits.

If

you

win...

well,

we'll

talk

about

that

if

you're

still

breathing."

[NEW QUEST: THE PIT’S FIRST TALLY]
[OBJECTIVE: Win three consecutive matches.]
[REWARD: 500 Credits, 200 XP, and 'Battle Mage' Rank Up.]
Kaelen looked at the cage. The Butcher was already inside, roaring as he tore the shirt off a Tier
2

challenger.

The

man

was

a

mountain

of

muscle,

his

skin

mottled

with

the

purple

bruising

of

a

Tier

3

"Hypertrophy"

Aspect.

Kaelen stepped through the gate. The laughter of the crowd hit him like a physical wave.
"A Null?!" someone screamed. "He's just a snack!"
The Butcher turned, his small, red-rimmed eyes fixing on Kaelen. He grinned, revealing rows of
yellowed

teeth.

"Fresh

meat,"

he

rumbled.

Kaelen didn't smile. He didn't even look afraid. He just raised his hands, and beneath the black
iron

ring,

the

blue

light

of

the

Battle

Mage

began

to

stir.
