The surface of West Salem was a jagged wound of a city, a place where the pre-Anomaly
architecture

of

brick

and

mortar

had

been

colonized

by

glowing

mana-pipes

and

rusted

steel

scaffolding.

The

"Grey

Zone"

was

the

buffer

between

the

industrial

hell

of

the

mines

and

the

pristine

floating

spires

of

the

Upper

Tiers.

Here,

the

air

was

a

thick,

yellow

soup—a

mixture

of

mountain

fog

and

the

toxic

exhaust

of

mana-refineries.

Kaelen moved through the crowds like a shadow. He had pulled his soot-stained hood over his
head,

but

his

heart

was

hammering

a

frantic

rhythm

against

his

ribs.

Every

pair

of

glowing

eyes

in

the

street

felt

like

a

spotlight.

In

this

world,

the

Awakened

didn't

just

have

power;

they

had

presence.

You

could

feel

a

Tier

3's

mana-pressure

from

across

a

street;

it

felt

like

a

static

charge

in

the

air.

Kaelen, however, felt like a void.
System, he thought, ducking into a narrow alleyway that smelled of wet copper and ozone. How
do

I

hide?

If

an

Enforcer

with

a

Sensory

Aspect

walks

by,

they’ll

see

my

mana-channels

lighting

up

like

a

Christmas

tree.

[SYSTEM NOTIFICATION]
[HOST IS CURRENTLY RADIATING UNSTABLE ENERGY.]
[RECOMMENDATION: ACCESS SYSTEM STORE.]
Kaelen leaned against a damp brick wall, closing his eyes. The blue interface flickered to life.
> [THE ASCENSION STORE - TIER 1]
> Current Credits: 50
> Available Items:
> * Shattered Mana Core (Consumable): Restores 50 MP. Cost: 20 Credits.
> * Basic Combat Tunic (Common): +5 Defense. Cost: 100 Credits.
> * Cloak of the Unseen (Rare): Dampens mana signature. Cost: 500 Credits.
> [Notice: Host has 1 "New User" Token. Would you like to redeem it now?]
>
"Redeem it," Kaelen whispered. "Please, give me something useful."
A violet box materialized in his mental vision, spinning with a low hum until it burst into
fragments

of

digital

light.

[REWARD: Ring of the Silent Pulse (Uncommon)]
* Type: Accessory / Concealment Gear.
* Effect: Passively masks the Host’s Level, Class, and Mana Signature from anyone below Tier
5.

* Bonus: +2 Intelligence.
Kaelen felt a cold, metallic weight manifest on his right ring finger. A simple band of black iron,
etched

with

runes

that

seemed

to

swallow

the

light.

The

moment

he

twisted

it,

the

buzzing

energy

in

his

veins

went

quiet.

He

still

felt

the

power—the

reservoir

of

strength

in

his

core—but

the

"leakage"

was

gone.

To

anyone

looking

at

him

now,

he

was

just

another

weary

miner,

a

Null

at

the

end

of

a

long

shift.

He let out a breath he didn't know he was holding. He wasn't just a fugitive; he was a ghost.
