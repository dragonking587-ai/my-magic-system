The world snapped back into motion with a violent jolt.
Barrett’s boot was inches from Kaelen’s face, carrying the full weight of a Tier 2 Awakened's
contempt.

In

the

past,

Kaelen

would

have

curled

into

a

ball.

He

would

have

taken

the

beating,

waited

for

the

pain

to

subside,

and

gone

back

to

work.

But as the "Aegis Ward" skill burned into his mind, he felt a reservoir of power opening in the
base

of

his

spine.

It

was

a

cold,

rushing

torrent

that

flooded

his

veins,

erasing

the

exhaustion

of

a

twelve-hour

shift

in

a

heartbeat.

"No," Kaelen said.
He flicked his wrist upward.
Aegis Ward.
A shimmering, translucent blue hexagonal grid erupted from Kaelen’s skin, forming a perfect
dome

around

him.

Barrett’s

boot

slammed

into

the

barrier.

The sound was like a sledgehammer hitting a concrete wall. Instead of Kaelen’s skull shattering,
it

was

Barrett

who

screamed.

The

kinetic

energy

of

the

kick

bounced

off

the

ward

and

traveled

straight

back

into

the

guard's

shin.

CRACK.
"AAAAAGH!" Barrett recoiled, clutching his leg as he collapsed into the dirt. "My leg! The
Null—he...

he

has

an

Aspect?!

Guards!

GUARDS!"

The other miners stopped. The silence that followed was heavier than the rock above them. A
Null

with

an

Aspect

was

an

impossibility,

a

heresy

against

the

natural

order

of

the

post-2006

world.

Kaelen stood up slowly. He felt taller, his senses sharpened to a razor edge. He could hear the
faint

hum

of

the

mana-shards

on

the

floor;

he

could

feel

the

heat

of

Barrett’s

fear.

"It's not an Aspect, Barrett," Kaelen said, his voice calm, almost predatory.
He extended his right hand. Spectral Blade.
With a low hum that vibrated the very air, particles of blue light coalesced in his palm. They knit
together,

forming

a

long,

straight

blade

of

energy.

"I'm finished with my shift," Kaelen said.
He swung the blade. It hissed through the air, cutting through Barrett’s defensive fire like it was
smoke.

The

overseer

fell,

and

as

Kaelen

turned

toward

the

elevator,

a

new

notification

flickered

in

the

corner

of

his

eye.
